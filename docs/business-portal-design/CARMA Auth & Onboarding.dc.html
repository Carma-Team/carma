Authentication & Business Onboarding

Flow 01 of the CARMA Business Portal, built on the approved Style Guide v2. Hebrew-first, RTL-first. Eighteen screens across three sub-flows: login & recovery, registration, and business onboarding — including every validation, loading, error and success state.

Brand intensity: road photography on auth screens only; onboarding forms stay clean and functional.
Rule honoured: the C symbol never sits on top of the road image — it appears alone on flat surfaces only.
Frames are 1280×800 (laptop viewport). Not yet designed: rewards, redemptions, analytics, dashboard.

The flow

Three entry paths converge on one gate: a business can only reach the portal once its details are submitted and approved.
