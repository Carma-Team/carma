Rewards Management

The first authenticated experience: the application shell, the rewards list, and one shared form for creating and editing a reward. Ten screens, Hebrew RTL, on the approved visual system. No road photography inside the portal — brand expression stopped at the door.

Revised against the real product model. Two capabilities that CARMA does not yet support are flagged NEW inline rather than drawn as existing behaviour.
Statuses: פעילה · מושהית · אזל המלאי · הסתיימה · בארכיון, plus מסתיימת בקרוב derived from the expiration date. No draft, no scheduled.
Frames are 1440×900. Out of scope: analytics, dashboard, employees, settings.
