Business Web Style Guide v2

Hebrew-first, RTL-first design system for the CARMA Business Portal — a desktop management application for business owners and staff. Same brand DNA as the driver app; its own product experience and UI patterns.

Language
All user-facing UI in Hebrew, RTL by default. Design-system terminology stays English.
Personality
Clean, modern, approachable, professional, efficient. Never gamified, never enterprise-gray.

New for the Business Portal

Rules & misuse
Logo stays indigo on white or near-white. On imagery, place it inside a white pill or use the reversed white version.
Road imagery always carries an indigo-to-navy overlay (≥55% at the text edge) so white text holds 4.5:1 contrast.
Don't recolor, outline, rotate or add effects to the logo; don't stretch it; don't rebuild the C from scratch as an icon.
No road image behind forms, tables, dense management content, or as a full-page background of the app shell.
The C is not a general-purpose icon: never in buttons, badges, table cells or as a bullet.
Never layer the C symbol over the road photography. Road imagery stands on its own with the indigo overlay; the C appears separately — logo, nav rail, loading, empty states, flat brand surfaces.
Production assets should ship as SVG with transparency (the supplied JPEGs are references) plus a white reversed logo for dark and photographic backgrounds.
Inherited from CARMAThe logo, the C symbol, indigo brand color, and the driving/road subject matter.
New for the portalA brand-intensity gradient — expressive at the entrance, near-silent inside the app — plus the split auth layout, watermark treatment and overlay contrast rules. The mobile app has no auth artwork or logo placement to inherit.

Hebrew typesetting rules. No uppercase exists in Hebrew — eyebrow labels use weight 700 + letter-spacing: .04em instead of text-transform, which is reserved for Latin strings only. Hebrew has no ascender/descender rhythm to lean on, so line-height runs one step looser than Latin (body 24 not 23). Never letter-space Hebrew body text. Use the geresh/gershayim (נק׳, ח״פ) rather than the ASCII apostrophe and quote.
InheritedTypeface, the extra-bold heading voice, muted secondary labels, and colored extra-bold metrics.
NewTitles drop 32→24px, a 13.5px dense tier + tabular numerals for tables, and explicit Hebrew typesetting rules the app never needed.

Validation checklist
Sidebar on the right; content scrolls on the left. Scrollbar sits on the left edge.
Labels, headings and body text align to start (right). Numeric table columns align to end (left).
Table columns read right→left, most important first; the row overflow menu sits at the far left.
In a footer button row the primary sits rightmost, cancel to its left.
Email, password, URL, phone and ID inputs are dir="ltr" with left-aligned text, but their Hebrew label stays right-aligned.
Use padding-inline, margin-inline-start, inset-inline, text-align: start; wrap Latin/numeric runs in an isolate so digits don't reorder.
Progress bars, charts and steppers fill right→left; time axes still run left→right (label them).
InheritedHebrew-first product language and the app's habit of keeping Latin brand names and numerals unlocalized.
NewEverything structural: RTL table direction, numeric end-alignment, LTR-isolated input fields, mirrored chevrons, left-edge overflow menus, and a written checklist to QA against.

Actions

Auto-width, 40px default height, radius 12. One primary per view region. In RTL, leading icons sit to the right of the label.

Destructive Button

Text Action & Icon Button

Sizes
Large (48) is reserved for authentication — registration and login — where a wide, confident CTA echoes the app. It never appears inside the management shell. Small (32) is for table toolbars and card row actions.
InheritedSolid indigo fill, white bold label, generous rounding, and the confident single-CTA feel — kept at 48px on auth screens only.
NewAuto width instead of full-bleed, a real hierarchy (secondary / destructive / text / icon-only), three sizes, and hover-focus-disabled-loading states the mobile app never defines.

Underline tabs, not the mobile indigo segmented pill: they scale past three items, carry counts, and read as in-page navigation rather than a toggle. Tab order starts at the right.
Filter bar
Pill chips keep the app's shape. Applied filters turn indigo-tinted with a removable ×; unapplied ones stay outlined dropdowns. Filter state persists in the URL so a filtered list can be shared with a colleague.

Modal / dialog

Radius 24, 32px padding, 480px (confirm) or 640px (form). Scrim rgba(15,23,42,.45).

למחוק את ההטבה "קפה גדול חינם"?
ההטבה תוסר מהאפליקציה ולא תוצג יותר לנהגים. 41 שוברים שטרם נוצלו יישארו בתוקף עד לתאריך התפוגה.
In RTL the primary sits at the right of the footer row, cancel to its left. Confirm dialogs only for irreversible actions. Reward create/edit is a full page, not a modal — too many fields plus an image upload. Long side tasks use a start-side drawer (480px).
